## Weapons

Properties: **Ammunition** (needs ammo, 1 free hand to load), **Finesse**
(use STR or DEX, same for attack+damage), **Heavy** (Disadvantage if
STR/DEX <13 as applicable), **Light** (enables an off-hand Bonus Action
attack, no ability mod to its damage unless negative), **Loading** (only
one shot per action/Bonus Action/Reaction regardless of attacks available),
**Range** (normal/long, Disadvantage beyond normal, can't hit beyond long),
**Reach** (+5 ft reach), **Thrown** (can throw for a ranged attack),
**Two-Handed**, **Versatile** (one- or two-handed, parenthetical = 2-handed
damage die).

Mastery properties (usable only with a Weapon-Mastery-granting feature —
Barbarian/Fighter/Paladin/Ranger/Rogue at level 1, see
`classes-and-subclasses.md`): **Cleave** (extra attack vs. a second nearby
target), **Graze** (min damage on a miss), **Nick** (Light's extra attack
folded into the Attack action itself), **Push** (10-ft shove on hit),
**Sap** (target has Disadvantage on its next attack), **Slow** (−10 ft
Speed on hit), **Topple** (CON save or Prone on hit), **Vex** (Advantage on
your next attack vs. that target).

## Gap-Reporting Note

The full Adventuring Gear list (individual torches, rope, tools, etc. sold
separately), Tools table, Mounts/Vehicles, and Magic Items sections of the
SRD are not transcribed here — they exist and are free/official, so consult
`character-creation.md`/`equipment.md` in
[downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown](https://github.com/downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown)
directly if a one-shot needs an item not covered above, rather than
inventing stats for it. Only flag a Step 10 gap for equipment that's
genuinely outside the SRD's scope (a named Player's Handbook magic item not
in the SRD's Magic Items section, for instance).
