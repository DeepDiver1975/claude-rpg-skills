# D&D 5e (2024) Species and Backgrounds

Source: System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast
LLC, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Summarized from `character-origins.md` in
[downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown](https://github.com/downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown).

**Important 2024-rules change from earlier editions**: ability score
increases now come from **Background**, not Species. Species grants creature
type (always Humanoid for every SRD species), size, Speed, and special
traits only — no ability score bonus. Don't default to 2014-era assumptions
(e.g. "+2 DEX for Elf") when building a character; use the background's
ability-score grant instead.

The SRD includes exactly **4 backgrounds** and **9 species**. A request for
any other background/species (e.g. "Guard" background, "Aasimar" or
"Genasi" species) is a Step 10 gap — flag it rather than improvising stats.

## Backgrounds

Every background grants: +2 to one listed ability and +1 to another (or +1
to all three listed), one Origin feat, proficiency in 2 listed skills, one
tool proficiency, and a choice of starting-equipment package or 50 GP.

| Background | Ability scores (pick +2/+1 split or +1/+1/+1) | Origin feat | Skill proficiencies | Tool proficiency |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Acolyte | INT, WIS, CHA | Magic Initiate (Cleric) | Insight, Religion | Calligrapher's Supplies |
| Criminal | DEX, CON, INT | Alert | Sleight of Hand, Stealth | Thieves' Tools |
| Sage | CON, INT, WIS | Magic Initiate (Wizard) | Arcana, History | Calligrapher's Supplies |
| Soldier | STR, DEX, CON | Savage Attacker | Athletics, Intimidation | One Gaming Set of choice |

Equipment packages (choice A) are listed in `equipment.md`; choice B is
always 50 GP instead.

## Species

All 9 SRD species are Humanoid creature type. Size/Speed/traits below;
no ability-score bonus (see note above).

| Species | Size | Speed | Notable traits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dragonborn | Medium | 30 ft | Choose a Draconic Ancestry (sets damage type); Breath Weapon (Cone/Line, DC 8+CON mod+PB, uses = PB/Long Rest); Damage Resistance to that type; Darkvision 60 ft; Draconic Flight (Bonus Action flight, from level 5) |
| Dwarf | Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision 120 ft; Resistance to Poison + Advantage vs. Poisoned; +1 max HP per level; Stonecunning (Bonus Action Tremorsense 60 ft on stone, uses = PB/Long Rest) |
| Elf | Medium | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft; choose an Elven Lineage (Drow/High Elf/Wood Elf) granting a cantrip + scaling bonus spells at levels 3/5; Advantage vs. Charmed; proficiency in Insight, Perception, or Survival; Trance (4-hour Long Rest, no sleep) |
| Gnome | Small | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft; Advantage on INT/WIS/CHA saves; choose Forest Gnome (Minor Illusion + always-prepared Speak with Animals) or Rock Gnome (Mending + Prestidigitation, can build a Tiny clockwork device) |
| Goliath | Medium | 35 ft | Choose a Giant Ancestry benefit (teleport/fire/cold/prone/damage-reduction/thunder, uses = PB/Long Rest); Large Form (Bonus Action size increase, from level 5); Advantage to end Grappled, counts as one size larger for carrying capacity |
| Halfling | Small | 30 ft | Advantage vs. Frightened; can move through larger creatures' spaces; Luck (reroll a natural 1, must use new roll); can Hide behind a creature only one size larger |
| Human | Medium or Small (choose) | 30 ft | Heroic Inspiration each Long Rest; proficiency in one skill of choice; one Origin feat of choice (Skilled recommended) |
| Orc | Medium | 30 ft | Adrenaline Rush (Bonus Action Dash + temp HP = PB, uses = PB/rest); Darkvision 120 ft; Relentless Endurance (drop to 1 HP instead of 0, once/Long Rest) |
| Tiefling | Medium or Small (choose) | 30 ft | Darkvision 60 ft; choose a Fiendish Legacy (Abyssal/Chthonic/Infernal) granting a cantrip + resistance + scaling bonus spells at levels 3/5; always knows *Thaumaturgy* |

For a level-1 pregen, only grant the species' level-1-tier trait benefits
(the table above already reflects that); any level-3/5-gated bonus spell or
level-5-gated ability (Dragonborn's Draconic Flight, Elf/Tiefling's bonus
spells, Goliath's Large Form) only applies if the GM opts for a
higher-level start (SKILL.md Step 1).
