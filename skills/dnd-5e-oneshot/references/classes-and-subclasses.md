# D&D 5e (2024) Classes and Subclasses

Source: System Reference Document 5.2.1 ("SRD 5.2.1") by Wizards of the Coast
LLC, licensed under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).
Summarized from `classes.md` in
[downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown](https://github.com/downfallx/dnd-5e-srd-markdown)
(itself a CC BY 4.0 Markdown conversion of the official SRD). Each subclass's
German name is sourced from the official German SRD PDF — see
`german-terminology.md` and `extraction-map-de.md`.

**Every one of the SRD's 12 classes gains its subclass at level 3, with no
exceptions** — this is uniform across all 12 classes in the 2024 rules (a
change worth calling out since some earlier editions granted certain
classes their subclass at level 1). This skill's default one-shot uses
level-1 pregens (SKILL.md Step 1), so **subclass features normally don't
apply yet** — still assign each PC the SRD's subclass below as intended
narrative identity/flavor (it's the class's signature specialization), but
don't grant its level-3+ mechanical features unless the GM opts for a
level-3-or-higher start. The SRD provides **exactly one subclass per class**;
a GM request for any other published subclass (Eldritch Knight instead of
Champion, Circle of the Moon instead of Circle of the Land, etc.) is this
skill's primary Step 10 gap-report trigger.

For each class below: role, Primary Ability, Hit Point Die, Saving Throw
Proficiencies, a couple of representative skill choices (full choice list is
in the SRD), and its three level-1 features paraphrased. Then its one SRD
subclass, tagline, and level-3 headline feature.

## Barbarian — melee striker/tank
Primary Ability STR · d12 HP · Saves STR/CON · skills: choose 2 from
Animal Handling, Athletics, Intimidation, Nature, Perception, Survival ·
weapons: Simple and Martial · armor: Light/Medium + Shields.
- **Rage** (Bonus Action, limited uses/rest): resistance to bludgeoning/
  piercing/slashing damage, bonus damage on STR attacks, advantage on STR
  checks/saves, can't cast spells or concentrate while raging.
- **Unarmored Defense**: AC = 10 + DEX mod + CON mod while unarmored.
- **Weapon Mastery**: can use the mastery property of 2 chosen weapon types.

Subclass: **Path of the Berserker** (German: **Pfad des Berserkers**) — *"Channel Rage into Violent Fury."*
Directs Rage toward pure violence and battlefield chaos. Headline (L3
Frenzy): extra weapon-type damage dice on the first hit each turn while
raging and using Reckless Attack.

## Bard — support caster/skill generalist
Primary Ability CHA · d8 HP · Saves DEX/CHA · skills: choose any 3 · tools:
choose 3 musical instruments · weapons: Simple · armor: Light.
- **Bardic Inspiration**: give an ally a d6 they can add to one check/save/
  attack roll.
- **Spellcasting**: CHA-based, full caster progression.

Subclass: **College of Lore** (German: **Schule des Wissens**) — *"Plumb the Depths of Magical Knowledge."*
Collectors of spells and secrets. Headline (L3 Bonus Proficiencies):
proficiency in 3 additional skills of choice.

## Cleric — primary healer/support caster
Primary Ability WIS · d8 HP · Saves WIS/CHA · skills: choose 2 from History,
Insight, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion · weapons: Simple · armor:
Light/Medium + Shields.
- **Spellcasting**: WIS-based, full caster progression.
- **Divine Order**: choose Protector (martial-weapon/heavy-armor training) or
  Thaumaturge (extra cantrip + Religion-check bonus) at level 1.

Subclass: **Life Domain** (German: **Domäne des Lebens**) — *"Soothe the Hurts of the World."* Masters of
healing magic. Headline (L3 Disciple of Life): healing spells restore extra
HP equal to 2 + the spell slot's level.

## Druid — nature caster
Primary Ability WIS · d8 HP · Saves INT/WIS · skills: choose 2 from Animal
Handling, Arcana, Insight, Medicine, Nature, Perception, Religion, Survival ·
tools: Herbalism Kit · weapons: Simple · armor: Light + Shields.
- **Spellcasting**: WIS-based, full caster progression.
- **Druidic**: knows the secret Druidic language; always has *Speak with
  Animals* prepared.
- **Primal Order**: choose Magician (extra cantrip + Arcana/Nature bonus) or
  Warden (martial-weapon/medium-armor training) at level 1.

(Wild Shape is a level-2 feature in the 2024 rules, not level 1 — don't grant
it to a level-1 pregen.)

Subclass: **Circle of the Land** (German: **Zirkel des Landes**) — *"Celebrate Connection to the Natural
World."* Sages who safeguard primal knowledge. Headline (L3 Circle of the
Land Spells): pick a land type (arid/polar/temperate/tropical) each Long
Rest for bonus always-prepared spells.

## Fighter — pure martial damage/tank
Primary Ability STR or DEX · d10 HP · Saves STR/CON · skills: choose 2 from
Acrobatics, Animal Handling, Athletics, History, Insight, Intimidation,
Persuasion, Perception, Survival · weapons: Simple and Martial · armor:
Light/Medium/Heavy + Shields.
- **Fighting Style**: a combat feat of choice (Defense recommended for a
  pregen).
- **Second Wind** (Bonus Action, limited uses/rest): heal 1d10 + Fighter
  level.
- **Weapon Mastery**: mastery property of 3 chosen weapon types.

Subclass: **Champion** (German: **Champion**) — *"Pursue Physical Excellence in Combat."* Relentless
pursuit of martial victory. Headline (L3 Improved Critical): scores a
critical hit on a roll of 19–20, not just 20.

## Monk — mobile unarmed/light-weapon striker
Primary Ability DEX and WIS · d8 HP · Saves STR/DEX · skills: choose 2 from
Acrobatics, Athletics, History, Insight, Religion, Stealth · tools: choose
one artisan's tool or musical instrument · weapons: Simple + Martial weapons
with the Light property · armor: none (relies on Unarmored Defense).
- **Martial Arts**: proficiency-grade unarmed strikes/monk weapons using
  DEX, plus a bonus unarmed strike.
- **Unarmored Defense**: AC = 10 + DEX mod + WIS mod while unarmored and
  shieldless.

Subclass: **Warrior of the Open Hand** (German: **Krieger der Offenen Hand**) — *"Master Unarmed Combat
Techniques."* Push/trip techniques and self-protection. Headline (L3 Open
Hand Technique): Flurry of Blows hits can also Addle, Push, or Topple the
target.

## Paladin — melee tank/support hybrid
Primary Ability STR and CHA · d10 HP · Saves WIS/CHA · skills: choose 2 from
Athletics, Insight, Intimidation, Medicine, Persuasion, Religion · weapons:
Simple and Martial · armor: Light/Medium/Heavy + Shields.
- **Lay On Hands**: a healing pool (5 × Paladin level HP) usable as an
  action.
- **Spellcasting**: CHA-based, half-caster progression.
- **Weapon Mastery**: mastery property of 2 chosen weapon types.

Subclass: **Oath of Devotion** (German: **Schwur der Hingabe**) — *"Uphold the Ideals of Justice and Order."*
The knight-in-shining-armor archetype. Headline (L3 Oath of Devotion
Spells): always-prepared bonus spells at set Paladin levels.

## Ranger — martial/caster hybrid, wilderness specialist
Primary Ability DEX and WIS · d10 HP · Saves STR/DEX · skills: choose 3 from
Animal Handling, Athletics, Insight, Investigation, Nature, Perception,
Stealth, Survival · weapons: Simple and Martial · armor: Light/Medium +
Shields.
- **Spellcasting**: WIS-based, half-caster progression.
- **Favored Enemy**: always has *Hunter's Mark* prepared, castable twice/day
  without a spell slot.
- **Weapon Mastery**: mastery property of 2 chosen weapon types.

Subclass: **Hunter** (German: **Jäger**) — *"Protect Nature and People from Destruction."*
Headline (L3 Hunter's Lore): while a target is marked by *Hunter's Mark*,
learn its immunities/resistances/vulnerabilities.

## Rogue — skill/stealth specialist, burst damage
Primary Ability DEX · d8 HP · Saves DEX/INT · skills: choose 4 from
Acrobatics, Athletics, Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Investigation,
Perception, Persuasion, Sleight of Hand, Stealth · tools: Thieves' Tools ·
weapons: Simple + Martial weapons with Finesse or Light · armor: Light.
- **Expertise**: double Proficiency Bonus on 2 chosen skills.
- **Sneak Attack**: once/turn, +1d6 damage on a Finesse/Ranged attack made
  with Advantage.
- **Thieves' Cant** + a bonus language.
- **Weapon Mastery**: mastery property of 2 chosen weapon types.

Subclass: **Thief** (German: **Dieb**) — *"Hunt for Treasure as a Classic Adventurer."* Headline
(L3 Fast Hands): Bonus Action Sleight of Hand check, Utilize/Magic-item-use
action, or Disarm/Pick Lock with Thieves' Tools.

## Sorcerer — innate blaster caster
Primary Ability CHA · d6 HP · Saves CON/CHA · skills: choose 2 from Arcana,
Deception, Insight, Intimidation, Persuasion, Religion · weapons: Simple ·
armor: none.
- **Spellcasting**: CHA-based, full caster progression.
- **Innate Sorcery** (Bonus Action, limited uses/rest, 1 minute): +1 to
  Sorcerer spell save DC and Advantage on Sorcerer spell attack rolls.

Subclass: **Draconic Sorcery** (German: **Drakonische Zauberei**) — *"Breathe the Magic of Dragons."* Innate
magic from a draconic bloodline/source. Headline (L3 Draconic Resilience):
+3 max HP (scaling with level) and unarmored AC bonus from dragon-like
scales.

## Warlock — pact-based caster
Primary Ability CHA · d8 HP · Saves WIS/CHA · skills: choose 2 from Arcana,
Deception, History, Intimidation, Investigation, Nature, Religion · weapons:
Simple · armor: Light.
- **Eldritch Invocations**: one invocation of choice (e.g. Pact of the
  Tome).
- **Pact Magic**: CHA-based casting with its own short-rest-recovering
  slot pool (distinct from other casters' long-rest recovery).

Subclass: **Fiend Patron** (German: **Unhold-Schutzherr**) — *"Make a Deal with the Lower Planes."* A pact
with a demon lord, archdevil, or similarly mighty fiend. Headline (L3 Dark
One's Blessing): gain temporary HP whenever an enemy near you drops to 0 HP.

## Wizard — versatile prepared caster
Primary Ability INT · d6 HP · Saves INT/WIS · skills: choose 2 from Arcana,
History, Insight, Investigation, Medicine, Nature, Religion · weapons:
Simple · armor: none.
- **Spellcasting**: INT-based, full caster progression, spells prepared
  from a spellbook.
- **Ritual Adept**: cast any prepared Ritual-tagged spell as a ritual
  straight from the spellbook, without preparing it.
- **Arcane Recovery** (1/Short Rest): recover expended spell slots (combined
  level ≤ half Wizard level rounded up, none level 6+).

Subclass: **Evoker** (German: **Hervorrufer**) — *"Create Explosive Elemental Effects."* Specializes
in bitter cold, flame, thunder, lightning, and acid magic. Headline (L3
Evocation Savant): 2 free Evocation spells (level ≤2) added to the
spellbook, plus ongoing free Evocation spells as new slot levels unlock.

## Balanced Party Selection

Reframes the Cyberpunk RED skill's Role-balance heuristic around 5e's class
archetypes: always include **≥1 tank-capable class** (Fighter, Paladin,
Barbarian), **≥1 healer/support class** (Cleric, Druid, Bard, or a
Lay-on-Hands Paladin), with remaining party slots filled by
**damage-strikers** (Rogue, Ranger, Monk, Sorcerer, Warlock) or
**utility-casters** (Wizard, Bard, Druid) as the premise calls for. A 3-6
person party (SKILL.md Step 1) doesn't need one of every archetype — just
make sure nobody's left with zero tank and zero healer coverage, since a
level-1 party with neither tends to have a rough time.
